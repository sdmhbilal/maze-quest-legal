This Privacy Policy explains how RookMint Studios (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you download, install, or use the mobile game Maze Quest (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Introduction

Maze Quest is a single-player maze / puzzle game. You can play levels offline, earn stars and coins, unlock themes, use hints, and complete daily and weekly challenges.

The App does not require you to create a RookMint Studios account or log in with a Maze Quest username and password to play.

When online features are available on your device, the App may use:

  • Google AdMob for advertisements (interstitial and rewarded ads)
  • Google Play Games Services for optional sign-in and leaderboards

We do not operate our own game backend or user database for Maze Quest progress.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information stored only on your device

The App stores gameplay data locally on your device (using on-device storage such as app preferences), including:

  • Levels unlocked and cleared
  • Stars, best moves, and best clear times
  • Coins and hint tokens
  • Owned themes and selected theme
  • Settings (sound, music, vibration)
  • Daily / weekly / quest progress and streaks
  • Counters used for in-game features (for example, how often an interstitial ad may appear)

This information is used to run the game on your device.
It is not uploaded to RookMint Studios servers, because we do not host a separate Maze Quest save server.

Uninstalling the App or clearing the App’s storage removes this local data from your device.

2.2 Information processed by Google Play Games Services (optional)

When Google Play Games Services is available, Maze Quest may automatically attempt to authenticate you with Google Play Games. Play Games sign-in is not required to play the core game. If authentication fails or is unavailable, you can still play.

If authentication succeeds, Google may process information related to your Google / Play Games profile, such as:

  • Play Games player identifier
  • Authentication / sign-in status
  • Leaderboard score we submit (number of levels cleared)

Google Play Games may also process profile information (for example a display name) and may show that information within Google Play Games interfaces, such as leaderboard screens. Maze Quest itself does not fetch or display your avatar.

We use Play Games to:

  • Attempt authentication at app start when the service is available
  • Submit and show leaderboard rankings when you are signed in
  • Open the Play Games leaderboard UI (which may prompt sign-in again if you were not already signed in)

If you are not signed in, leaderboard features may be unavailable, but core maze gameplay remains available.

Google Play Games is provided by Google LLC. Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s policies (see Section 7).

2.3 Information processed by Google AdMob (advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob to show:

  • Rewarded ads (for example, to earn a free hint)
  • Interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown after a set number of play attempts, such as wins, losses, or retries)

When ads are loaded or shown, Google AdMob and related Google advertising services may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) or similar identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Device and app information (for example device model, OS version, language, app version, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewarded ad completions)
  • Diagnostic information related to ad serving

This information is used to:

  • Serve and measure ads
  • Support rewarded ads
  • Limit or personalize ads according to your settings and applicable law
  • Detect fraud and keep advertising reliable

We do not sell your personal information. Google may use advertising data under Google’s policies and your device / account ad settings.

2.4 Information we do not collect through the App

Based on the current App implementation, we do not ask for or intentionally collect through Maze Quest gameplay:

  • Email address (except if you choose to email us for support)
  • Phone number
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts
  • Photos, camera, or microphone content
  • Payment / financial information inside the App (there is no in-app purchase system in the current build)

We also do not operate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Unity Analytics, or a custom analytics backend in the current App.

3. Information Collected Automatically

When the App contacts Google services (AdMob and, if used, Play Games), those services may automatically receive technical data such as IP address, device identifiers, and device/app metadata needed to provide ads or social/leaderboard features.

The App itself does not implement a separate first-party analytics or crash-reporting pipeline.

4. How We Use Information

We and our service providers (primarily Google) use information to:

  1. Provide and operate the App (progress, themes, challenges, hints, settings)
  2. Show AdMob ads, including rewarded ads
  3. Enable optional Play Games leaderboards
  4. Remember your preferences on the device
  5. Prevent abuse and technical issues related to ads and online Google features
  6. Comply with law and Google Play / Google product policies
  7. Respond if you contact us for support

5. Advertising

Advertising in Maze Quest is powered by Google AdMob.

Current ad formats in the App:

  • Rewarded ads
  • Interstitial ads

Banner ads are not implemented in the current game code.

You can typically manage advertising preferences through:

  • Android Advertising ID / ads personalization settings on your device
  • Google Ads Settings

6. Google Play Games Services

When Google Play Games Services is available, Maze Quest may automatically attempt to authenticate you with Google Play Games at launch. Play Games sign-in is not required to play the core game.

When signed in, Maze Quest may:

  • Submit a levels-cleared score to a leaderboard
  • Open the Play Games leaderboard UI

Opening leaderboards may trigger a manual Play Games sign-in flow if you are not already authenticated.

Achievements, cloud saved games, and Nearby Connections features are not used by Maze Quest game code. Maze Quest does not fetch or display your Play Games avatar; any profile details shown in leaderboards appear through Google Play Games interfaces.
